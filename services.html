01 / 06 · IEC 62676

CCTV Surveillance

IP and analog camera systems, NVR/DVR infrastructure, video management and storage planning for perimeter, indoor and critical-area monitoring — from site survey to as-built documentation.

  • IP megapixel, PTZ, fisheye and thermal cameras
  • Analog HD (HDCVI/TVI) for retrofits
  • NVR/DVR with RAID storage design
  • VMS integration — Milestone, Genetec, Hikvision, Dahua
  • AutoCAD layouts, cable schedules and BOQ
  • Compliant with IEC 62676

02 / 06 · EDGE / SERVER AI

Intelligent Video Analytics

AI-driven analytics that turn passive CCTV into an active security layer, reducing operator fatigue with actionable alerts — deployed at the edge or on server.

  • Perimeter intrusion & virtual tripwire
  • Loitering, crowd density and abandoned object alerts
  • LPR / ANPR
  • Face recognition & people counting
  • AI fire & smoke detection
  • Integration with PAGA and access control for automated response

03 / 06 · NFPA 72 · BS 5839 · EN 54

Fire Alarm & PA Systems

Addressable and conventional fire alarm systems and PAGA for evacuation and area communication — for industrial plants, petrochemical facilities, hotels and commercial buildings.

  • Addressable FAS — Notifier, Hochiki, Siemens, Apollo compatible
  • Loop-based panel design with point-by-point device mapping
  • PAGA amplifier sizing, zone routing and speaker tapping calculations
  • Voice Evacuation — EN 54-16, NFPA 72 Ch. 24
  • Integration with suppression, fire doors and BMS
  • Single-line diagrams, zone schedules and BOQ

04 / 06 · CARD · BIOMETRIC · INTERCOM

Access Control Systems

Card, biometric and intercom-integrated solutions with central management — from single-entry offices to multi-gate industrial facilities.

  • IP controllers — HID, Lenel, Honeywell, ZKTeco, Suprema
  • MIFARE, DESFire, HID iCLASS and proximity credentials
  • Fingerprint, face and iris biometrics
  • Turnstiles, boom barriers and vehicle access
  • Fail-safe vs fail-secure hardware specification
  • AutoCAD drawings, device schedules and BOQ

05 / 06 · TIA-568 · ISO/IEC 11801

Data Networks & WiFi Infra

Structured cabling, core/edge switching, wireless planning and telecom room builds — to TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801.

  • Cat6/Cat6A copper; OS2, OM3 and OM4 fibre
  • MDF/IDF rack layouts, power and patching schedules
  • Cisco, HPE Aruba and Ubiquiti switching
  • Enterprise WiFi site survey, AP placement and channel planning
  • VLAN design and OT/IT segmentation
  • Certified test reports
