Core Principles

Three rules we don't bend.

01 NFPA · EN · IEC · TIA

Code Compliance First

Every design is engineered to the applicable international standards and local authority requirements from day one — so approvals are a formality, not a redesign.

02 DWG · BOQ · AS-BUILT

Accurate Documentation

AutoCAD drawings, BOQs, device schedules and as-builts that contractors can build from and owners can maintain against for the life of the system.

03 OWNER · PMC · EPC
